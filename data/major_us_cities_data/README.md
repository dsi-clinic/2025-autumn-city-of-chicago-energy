 # Major US Cities Energy Benchmarking Data

This folder contains building energy benchmarking datasets from several major U.S. cities.

---

## Contents

### **San Francisco (SF)**
Source: https://data.sfgov.org/Energy-and-Environment/Existing-Buildings-Energy-Performance-Ordinance-Re/96ck-qcfe/about_data
San Francisco’s dataset contains 28,243 rows covering 1,523 distinct buildings with 14 years of reporting (2011–2024).
It includes ENERGY STAR Score, Site and Source EUI, GHG emissions metrics, and fuel usage. This dataset represents one of the longest-running and most comprehensive local energy benchmarking programs in the U.S.

---

### **Seattle**
Source: https://data.seattle.gov/Built-Environment/Benchmarking-Performance-Ranges-by-Building-Type-2/sfav-5thy/about_data
The Seattle dataset contains 24,699 rows spanning 3,013 buildings with approximately 10 years of reporting (2015–present).
It includes ENERGY STAR Score, Site EUI, Source EUI, and related performance metrics, primarily reported in aggregated or category-based formats.

---

### **Boston**
Source: https://data.boston.gov/dataset/building-emissions-reduction-and-disclosure-ordinance
Boston’s benchmarking data are published as year-specific files, primarily in Excel format, with earlier years (2017–2018) available as CSVs. The dataset spans reporting years from 2015 to 2025 and includes building-level information such as ENERGY STAR Score, Site Energy Use, natural gas consumption, and emissions-related metrics.
